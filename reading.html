Reading

This is meant to be a record of the reading I do. There is frequently a lot more reading that I do that isn’t books, but I think the remarkable thing about a book, at least historically, is that it’s meant to represent that the author tried very hard. This barrier is lower for blogs, Substacks, and tweets, and the quality is usually much lower. So I have a bit of a flight to safety in that books are generally a much higher-quality information environment.
